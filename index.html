How to use the wind

Wind direction is the direction the wind comes from. For example, a NE wind carries scent toward SW. Compare the “Scent TO” column with the deer approach and bedding areas at each stand. This forecast cannot determine stand safety without stand coordinates and approach routes.

The rut timing is a broad mid-November western Kentucky assumption, not a live biological measurement. The model is a heuristic for ranking hours; it does not know individual bucks, hunting pressure, camera observations, bedding cover, or your stand locations. Forecast wind at 10 m may differ substantially from wind and thermals in wooded hollows. Do not use the score as a literal percentage chance of seeing a buck.
